# Connectivity Tests — Milestone 2

## Test Objective
Verify that inter-VLAN routing works correctly across all departments through the L3 core switch (SW-CORE).

## Test Environment
- **Source:** PC-ADMIN (VLAN 10) — 172.30.84.2
- **Default Gateway:** 172.30.84.1 (SW-CORE VLAN 10 SVI)

## Test Procedure
From PC-ADMIN's Command Prompt, four ICMP packets were sent to each destination using the `ping` command.

## Results

| Destination | IP Address | VLAN | Sent | Received | Lost | Status |
|-------------|-----------|------|------|----------|------|--------|
| PC-EDIT | 172.30.84.34 | 20 | 4 | 3 | 1 (25%) | ✅ Success |
| PC-SALES | 172.30.84.66 | 30 | 4 | 3 | 1 (25%) | ✅ Success |
| PC-DESIGN | 172.30.84.98 | 40 | 4 | 3 | 1 (25%) | ✅ Success |
| Server | 172.30.84.146 | 60 | 4 | 3 | 1 (25%) | ✅ Success |
| Boardroom Laptop | 172.30.84.130 | 50 | 4 | 3 | 1 (25%) | ✅ Success |

## Interpretation
The first packet of each ping shows `Request timed out` — this is the ARP (Address Resolution Protocol) process resolving the destination MAC address. The subsequent replies confirm successful inter-VLAN routing. This is expected behaviour and does not indicate a fault.
